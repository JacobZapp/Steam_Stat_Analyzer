Steam Stat Analyzer

Analyze your Steam profile, games, achievements, and playtime.

What should I enter?

Enter either your SteamID number or your Custom URL name.

To find these: Open Steam → click your profile name → Profile → Edit Profile.

If you have a Custom URL set, enter the name shown in the Custom URL box. For example, if your Custom URL is steamcommunity.com/id/johndoe, enter johndoe.

If you don't have a Custom URL, you can copy your Steam profile URL and paste it here instead.
